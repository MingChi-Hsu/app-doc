# MinWeather Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 4 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the MinWeather Android app ("the app"), developed by Mindy Hsu ("I", "me"), handles your information. MinWeather is a weather app that shows current conditions and a 5-day forecast for your current location or for a city you search for.

## Summary

- MinWeather does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
- If you allow location access, your device location is used only to fetch weather for where you are.
- I do not run my own servers, and I do not store, sell or share your data for advertising.
- The app contains no ads and no analytics.

## Information the app uses

### Location

If you grant location permission, the app requests your device's current location through Google Play services (the Fused Location Provider). It may use precise or approximate location, depending on what you allow.

Your latitude and longitude are sent to OpenWeatherMap only to retrieve the weather for that location. The app does not save your location on your device or anywhere else, and does not track your location in the background.

Location access is optional. The app only accesses your location with your consent, which you give by granting location permission, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings. If you deny it, you can still search for a city by name.

### City searches

When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to OpenWeatherMap's geocoding service to find matching places. Search history is not saved.

### Technical information

Like any app that connects to the internet, network requests to OpenWeatherMap include standard technical information such as your IP address. I do not collect or have access to this information.

## Third-party services

The app relies on the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

- **OpenWeatherMap** (weather data, city search and weather icons): <https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy>
- **Google Play services** (device location): <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Data storage and retention

I do not keep any of your data. Weather results are held in memory only while the app is open and are discarded when you close it.

## Security

All requests to OpenWeatherMap are made over encrypted HTTPS connections.

## Your choices and rights

You can turn location access on or off at any time in your device settings (Settings > Apps > MinWeather > Permissions). Uninstalling the app removes it and all of its data from your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because I do not store your personal data, there is usually nothing for me to provide or delete, but you are welcome to contact me with any request. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
